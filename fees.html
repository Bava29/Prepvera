Still Have A Question?
How do I choose the right plan?

Choose Starter if you prefer self-paced learning, Pro if you want complete preparation with regular practice and support, or Elite if you need personalised mentorship and guidance.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can move to a higher plan when your preparation needs change. Your learning journey does not have to stay limited to one plan.

Are mock tests included in every plan?

Mock tests are available across our plans, with the number and level of test access depending on the plan you choose.

Do the plans include study materials?

Yes. Study materials are included based on the selected plan and are designed to support your regular learning and revision.

Is personal mentorship included?

Personal mentorship and individual study guidance are available with the Elite plan.

How long will I have access?

Access duration depends on the plan or course you select. The applicable access period will be clearly shown before registration.
